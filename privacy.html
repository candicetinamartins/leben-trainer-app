Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 6, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Leben in Deutschland ("the App"). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our flashcard study application for the German citizenship test.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Language preference — stored locally on your device using localStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Usage data via ads — our third-party advertising partner (Google AdSense) may collect anonymised usage data, device identifiers, and cookies to serve relevant advertisements.

3. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this app or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

For more information on how Google uses your data, please review Google's advertising privacy policy.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

This app uses:

  • localStorage — to remember your selected language. This is essential for app functionality.
  • Third-party cookies — set by Google AdSense for ad personalisation. These can be managed via your browser settings or through our consent banner.

5. GDPR (European Economic Area Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), we will ask for your consent before serving personalised ads. You have the right to:

  • Withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser data or using the consent banner.
  • Access any personal data we hold (we store none server-side).
  • Request deletion of any data — since all data is stored locally, clearing your browser data accomplishes this.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ads Settings.

6. Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. All app preferences are stored locally on your device. Third-party ad data retention is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdSense — advertising (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts — font delivery (Privacy FAQ)

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
